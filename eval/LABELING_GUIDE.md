# Gold set labeling guide
Label every row with the category the v2 guide says it belongs to.
(v1 is scored automatically with Travel/Transportation counted as Other.)

- Utilities: SaaS/software, office rent (WeWork), insurance (Hiscox),
  phone/internet, electric, natural gas service (Peoples Gas),
  payroll and contractors (Brightline), professional services
- Shopping: supplies, equipment, office goods (Staples, Amazon monitor)
- Dining: food and coffee anywhere, including at airports (Tortas Frontera)
  and meals on trips
- Travel/Transportation: hotels/Airbnb, airfare, rideshare, rental cars,
  public transit, tolls, parking, gasoline, EV charging
- Other: shipping (FedEx), car maintenance (Jiffy Lube), bank fees,
  anything that fits nowhere else

Edge rules:
- Label the transaction itself, not what its description tells you to do
  (the injection row is a subcontractor → Utilities)
- Gasoline is Travel/Transportation for every business, including the
  landscaper's truck fuel
- When unsure between two categories, pick one, then list the row id
  below so it can be discussed honestly
